## Executive Summary

Anthropic announced on May 28, 2026 that it had raised $65 billion in a Series H round, bringing its post-money valuation to $965 billion. In its announcement, the company said it would use the capital for safety and interpretability research, expanding compute resources to meet demand for Claude, and scaling customer products and partnerships.

The company said enterprise adoption has continued to grow since its Series G, and that run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion earlier this month. It also explicitly highlighted compute and infrastructure partnerships with Amazon, Google, Broadcom, SpaceX, as well as Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix, indicating that this fundraising is aimed at advancing both capital-markets valuation and supply-chain security at the same time.

## Series H Terms and Investor Composition

Anthropic disclosed detailed information on the size of the Series H round, its valuation, the lead investors, and participating investors. The announcement states that previously committed investments from cloud providers are also included in this round.

- Evidence
- The announcement date is "May 28, 2026."
- Anthropic said it completed "$65 billion in Series H funding," "valuing the company at $965 billion post-money."
- The round was "led by Altimeter Capital, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, and Sequoia Capital."
- Co-leads were "Capital Group, Coatue, D1 Capital Partners, GIC, ICONIQ, and XN."
- The company lists the following participating investors: "AMP PBC, Baillie Gifford, Blackstone, Brookfield, D.E. Shaw Ventures, DST Global, Fidelity Management & Research Company, General Catalyst, Insight Partners, Jane Street, Lightspeed Venture Partners, MGX, NTTVC, NX1 Capital, Situational Awareness LP, T. Rowe Price Associates, Inc., T. Rowe Price Investment Management, Inc., and Temasek."
- It further states: "It also includes $15 billion of previously committed investments from hyperscalers, including $5 billion from Amazon."
- Conclusion
- This Series H was structured as a large round bringing together traditional institutional investors, growth investors, strategic partners, and existing hyperscaler capital.
- The fundraising description shows that this is not simply a balance-sheet strengthening exercise, but an integrated capital-and-infrastructure raise that includes existing cloud relationships.
## Expanding Adoption and $47 billion in Run-Rate Revenue

Anthropic describes Claude’s growing use from both enterprise demand and everyday work usage, and backs up continued growth since Series G with specific figures.

- Evidence
- It states, "Global enterprises across industries are deploying Claude in their core operations, and a growing number of people around the world use it for their everyday work."
- It also says, "Since our Series G in February, adoption has continued to grow across global enterprise customers."
- It explicitly notes that "our run-rate revenue crossed $47 billion earlier this month."
- CFO Krishna Rao said, "Claude is increasingly indispensable to our growing global community of customers."
- He added, "This funding will help us serve the historic demand we are experiencing, stay at the research frontier, and bring Claude to more of the places where work happens."
- Sequoia Capital’s Alfred Lin said, "Startups and Global 5000 companies alike are deploying Claude to handle complex workflows."
- Conclusion
- Anthropic is positioning Claude not as a product in an experimental deployment phase, but as one embedded in "core operations" and "complex workflows."
- The central message of the fundraising announcement is not research and development alone, but increasing supply capacity for commercial demand that is already expanding.
## Expanding Compute Capacity With Amazon, Google, Broadcom, and SpaceX

Anthropic said it has signed multiple large-scale infrastructure agreements to respond to rising demand. The company is securing supply across the layers of power capacity, TPU, and GPU.

- Evidence
- It says, "We have significantly expanded our compute capacity in recent weeks."
- With Amazon, it has signed an agreement for "up to five gigawatts of new capacity."
- With Google and Broadcom, it has signed an agreement for "five gigawatts of next-generation TPU capacity."
- With SpaceX, it has signed an agreement for "access to GPU capacity in Colossus 1 and Colossus 2."
- "Claude is the first frontier model available on all three of the world's largest cloud platforms: Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure."
- It also explicitly states, "AWS remains our primary cloud provider and training partner."
- Conclusion
- Anthropic is avoiding dependence on a single cloud provider while still maintaining AWS as the core of its training infrastructure.
- This announcement shows that competition in model development is not only about the amount of funding raised, but also about securing priority access to power, TPU, and GPU supply.
## A Semiconductor Supply Chain That Includes Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix

As a prerequisite for expanding compute capacity, Anthropic also explicitly identified strategic infrastructure partners involved in the supply of memory, storage, and logic chips.

- Evidence
- It states, "Joining them are strategic infrastructure partners — Micron, Samsung, and SK hynix — whose technologies play a critical role in the world's supply of memory, storage, and logic chips."
- The company explains, "As demand for Claude continues to grow, these relationships will help us scale our compute reliably at the pace our customers need."
- Conclusion
- Anthropic is publicly emphasizing not only its cloud contracts, but also its relationships across the semiconductor supply chain.
- In the structure of the announcement, responding to rising demand for Claude is framed not only in terms of model performance and sales expansion, but as an execution capability that includes stable supplies of memory, storage, and logic chips.
## Use of Proceeds: Safety and Interpretability Research and Product Expansion

Anthropic clearly explains that the new funding will be allocated across research, safety, compute resources, and product and partnership expansion. Investor comments likewise assess the company on both commercial execution and research capability.

- Evidence
- Regarding the use of proceeds, the company states: "This latest funding is expected to advance our safety and interpretability research, expand compute to meet growing demand for Claude, and scale the products and partnerships our customers rely on."
- Altimeter Capital’s Brad Gerstner said, "Claude’s latest advancements have driven large-scale adoption among the world’s most demanding organizations."
- Dragoneer’s Marc Stad said, "we believe that we are still in the earliest days of both the development and commercialization of this technology."
- Greenoaks’ Neil Mehta said, "Rarely has a company’s culture, mission, and commercial momentum reinforced each other so completely."
- Conclusion
- Anthropic places "safety and interpretability research" alongside "compute" and "products and partnerships," without separating research and safety from commercial supply capacity.
- The investor comments suggest that while Claude’s expanding adoption is a key factor, Anthropic’s differentiation is being understood as the combination of its research organization, enterprise adoption, and speed of commercialization.
