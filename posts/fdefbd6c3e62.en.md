## Anthropic Acquires Stainless, an SDK and MCP Server Tooling Company

## Executive Summary

On 2026-05-18, Anthropic announced the acquisition of Stainless, a leader in SDKs and MCP server tooling. The announcement positions the acquisition as an organizational integration designed to expand Claude’s ability to reach the data and tools it connects to, as the center of gravity in AI shifts from “models that answer” to “agents that act.”

Anthropic explicitly states that Stainless has powered the generation of “every official Anthropic SDK” since the earliest days of the API, which suggests this is less a new partnership than a move to bring an existing piece of developer infrastructure in-house and operate it as an integrated part of the company.

## The Official Framing of the Stainless Acquisition

Anthropic explains this announcement not as an expansion of model capability itself, but as an expansion of execution capability—specifically, which systems agents can connect to.

- Evidence
- The announcement date is “May 18, 2026”.
- Anthropic states, “Today, Anthropic is acquiring Stainless, a leader in SDKs and MCP server tooling, to extend that reach even further.”
- In the same context, it writes, “The frontier of AI is shifting from models that answer to agents that act—and agents are only as capable as the systems they can reach.”
- Conclusion
- The main focus of the acquisition is not the advancement of standalone models, but strengthening the connectivity of agents centered around Claude.
- Anthropic itself emphasizes “reach,” indicating that it sees developer infrastructure as part of the agent execution environment.
## The Anthropic SDK Foundation Stainless Has Been Supporting

The announcement makes clear that Stainless was not just a vendor of adjacent tools for Anthropic, but a core part of its existing SDK supply chain.

- Evidence
- Stainless was “Founded in 2022”.
- Anthropic explains, “Stainless has powered the generation of every official Anthropic SDK since the earliest days of our API.”
- It describes Stainless as follows: “Hundreds of companies rely on Stainless to generate SDKs, CLIs, and MCP servers—the libraries, command-line tools, and connectors that let developers and agents use an API.”
- The supported languages listed are “TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, and more”.
- Of the generated artifacts, it says, “Each one is fast, reliable, and built to feel native in its language.”
- Conclusion
- For Anthropic, Stainless is not merely a future investment target, but an established foundation that has already supported production SDK delivery.
- By placing SDKs, CLIs, and MCP servers in the same context, Anthropic is treating the API usage experience not simply as library distribution, but as a comprehensive connection layer that enables both developers and agents to use an API.
## From the Claude API to Claude’s Connection to “data and tools”

Comments from Anthropic executives broaden the purpose of the acquisition beyond improving the developer experience of the Claude API to expanding Claude’s own external connectivity.

- Evidence
- Katelyn Lesse, Head of Platform Engineering at Anthropic, said:
“This has shaped how developers experience the Claude API since the start, and it’s been great to work with them on that”

- She continued:
“Agents are only as useful as what they can connect to. We’re excited to bring the Stainless team into Anthropic to advance Claude’s ability to connect to data and tools.”

- Conclusion
- Anthropic is presenting this acquisition not only as an effort to improve SDK quality, but also as a measure to strengthen Claude’s ability to connect to “data and tools”.
- Because this comes from the Head of Platform Engineering, it can be interpreted not as a marketing message but as a statement about the connectivity architecture of the Claude Platform itself.
## Bringing Agent Connectivity In-House Around MCP

At the end of the announcement, Anthropic positions MCP as the foundation for agent connectivity and portrays the integration of the Stainless team as a driver of the Claude Platform.

- Evidence
- Anthropic states, “Anthropic created MCP to make agent connectivity possible.”
- It then adds, “By bringing together the Stainless and Anthropic teams, the Claude Platform continues to push the frontier of developer experience and agent connectivity.”
- Alex Rattray, Founder and CEO of Stainless, said:
“I started Stainless because SDKs deserve as much care as the APIs they wrap. Anthropic was one of the first teams to bet on this with us.”

- He further stated:
“We have been watching what developers have built on Claude over the last few years, which made bringing our teams together an easy decision. The team gets to keep doing the work we love, on the platform where it matters most.”

- Conclusion
- Anthropic is reaffirming MCP as a prerequisite for agent connectivity and signaling a structure in which surrounding tooling is being absorbed not through external partnership but through team integration.
- Alex Rattray’s comments suggest that Anthropic was also one of Stainless’s early customers, and that this acquisition is an extension of an existing collaboration rather than a wholly new relationship.
