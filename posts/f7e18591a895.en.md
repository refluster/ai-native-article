## Executive Summary

In its "AI Release Highlights Q3 2026," dated October 26, 2026, SAP says 45,000 cloud customers now use its AI solutions. The quarter's center of gravity is a structure in which domain-specific Joule Assistants orchestrate many specialized agents, which SAP presents as a step toward the "Autonomous Enterprise." This article is a reading of SAP's own announcement: every effectiveness figure below is either an SAP "estimated benefit" or a customer's own statement.

## The Skeleton: Joule Work, Assistants, Agents

SAP defines Joule as its portfolio-wide AI engagement layer. In **Joule Work**, the new UI for AI, a user describes what they want to achieve, and Joule Work selects the right **Joule Assistant**. Assistants act as domain experts with knowledge of roles and processes, and orchestrate specialized **Joule Agents**. The agents use business context and data, respect established authorizations, and maintain audit trails while executing the work.

In SAP's words: "People set the direction, assistants coordinate agents, and agents carry out work with proper oversight and governance."

Joule Work is available on desktop, in-app, web, and mobile. It works with local files and folders in a secure sandbox, integrates web search, and connects to SAP and non-SAP applications through Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools and Agent-to-Agent (a2a) integrations. Custom and corporate skills streamline repetitive tasks; where a skill is not enough, the SAP Knowledge Graph searches the entire space of business objects available to the user.

## Scale and Availability in Numbers

- **Customers**: 45,000 cloud customers use SAP's AI solutions.
- **Agent counts**: each domain assistant lists the agents released in Q3. For example, the Billing Assistant has six, the Financial Closing Assistant seven, the Accounts Receivable Assistant seven, and the Asset Operations Assistant six.
- **Availability**: for most assistants, general availability is "now underway in a phased approach." The Recurring Receivables Assistant, the Tax and Compliance Assistant, and the International Trade Assistant are available only through the SAP Early Adopter Care program.
- **Pricing**: the Joule Studio agent builder is free until December 2026, after which it becomes a premium AI feature. The skill builder is a base AI feature.
## What Is in Each Domain

**Supply chain (Autonomous SCM).** The Product Design Assistant has three agents (product data maintenance, bill-of-materials optimization, change record management); its estimated benefits are a 19% reduction in product design costs, a 10% increase in prototype success, and a five percent reduction in cost of goods sold from design improvements. The Manufacturing Assistant has four agents and estimates a 14% reduction in production cycle time, a 19% reduction in scrap and defect rates, and a six percent reduction in changeover time, among others. The Supply Chain Planning Assistant has three agents and estimates a 20% reduction in planning coordination overhead and a 10% reduction in planning cycle time. The Asset Operations Assistant has six agents covering maintenance from first request to final debrief.

**HR (Autonomous HCM).** The HR Service Assistant has six agents and estimates a 24% increase in case deflection to self-service, a 26% reduction in time to resolution, and a 40% reduction in queries needing Tier 2 or higher intervention. The HR Operations Assistant has six agents and estimates a 39% reduction in data errors, a 20% reduction in HR transactional work, and a 25% reduction in manager time on core HR workflows. The Performance and Goals Assistant includes a Calibration Equity Agent that identifies potential inconsistencies in performance ratings before or during calibration.

**Finance (Autonomous Finance).** The Billing Assistant covers invoice creation, anomaly detection, adjustments, and posting support; the Financial Closing Assistant covers journal entries, accruals, clearing, and analysis. SAP states explicitly that the latter lets teams control the extent to which agents participate in journal postings, clearing, and analysis. The Cash and Treasury, Financial Consolidation, Sales Operations, Accounts Receivable, and Accounts Payable assistants follow.

**Procurement (Autonomous Spend Management).** The Purchasing, Supplier Management, Procurement Contract, Category Management, Invoicing, and Receiving assistants.

The release also lists AI features inside existing applications as generally available: natural-language KPI and report summaries in SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition, EHS compliance management, aging work-in-progress analytics, and AI-assisted access review in SAP Cloud Identity Access Governance.

## What the Customer Examples Say

The customer statements SAP cites differ in the kind of evidence they offer.

- **Novartis**: Christoph Buerki, head of Procurement, said of the Sourcing Assistant pilot that he "expects improvements of 25% faster cycle times and a 35% uplift in FTE productivity." That is an expectation before deployment, not a result.
- **Reinhausen**: Peter Wied, director of Complexity Management, said that work which "once required up to an hour of manual effort across our SAP S/4HANA environment is now accessible in seconds through natural language," from the Joule Work desktop early adopter program.
- **KNAPP**: an assistant built in Joule Studio reduced issue resolution times by about 90% and time spent on documentation and training by about 80%, according to the report.
- **Wipro** reports a 40% reduction in development effort and a 25% decrease in software rework with Joule for Consultants. **SEIDOR** expects to reduce consultant training time by 20 to 30% and save 93 hours each week on information searches.
- **PwC** uses the Billing Assistant to identify potential issues earlier; no figures are given.
## Control Mechanisms: AI Agent Hub and Joule Studio

As agents multiply, the overall picture becomes the problem. SAP AI Agent Hub provides a central inventory of SAP and non-SAP agents to help teams keep track of and govern a growing portfolio. SAP Signavio agent mining and the Process Consulting Agent are described as helping customers identify their biggest opportunities for agentic AI and act on them right away.

Joule Studio is where customers build their own agents. The agent builder creates agents that plan, reason, and dynamically address business challenges. The skill builder creates reusable operations with predefined logic for retrieving data and executing transactions. SAP positions the former as the goal-driven option for ambiguity and exceptions and the latter as predictable, task-specific automation, and presents the combination as the design. The estimated benefits are a 35% reduction in the time to build and deploy custom Joule Agents and a 10% reduction in the time to deploy custom Joule skills. Built-in governance and centralized management of skills, agent execution, and data privacy enforcement are also listed as features.

## Assessment: What Is Established and What Is Not

**Established by the announcement**

- The three-layer structure (Joule Work above domain assistants above agents) is the product's official design.
- Most features are in phased general availability, and some are limited to an early adopter program.
- The agent builder becomes a paid feature after December 2026.
**Not determinable from this document**

- Most effectiveness figures are "estimated"; no calculation method or comparison baseline is given, and no independent verification is included.
- The document does not say how many of the 45,000 customers run agents in production.
- The scope of authority each agent actually operates under is not described agent by agent. Human review is stated explicitly only for some, such as the Material Reservation Processing Agent.
**Implication**: SAP's design assumes that the center of business software moves from screens to stated goals, and that what users operate is an assistant rather than an application. At the same time, inventory management through AI Agent Hub and the move of the agent builder to a paid tier show that running agents becomes both a managed asset and a billable one. The first decision for an adopting organization is which part of which process to hand to agents, and who audits their execution history.
