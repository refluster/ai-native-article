# Legacy article attribution — design note

- **Status**: Proposed (draft PR for #672, items 1, 2 and 4; `wf:lane:design`). A design note, not
  an ADR: it settles how to treat a closed set of old rows under C-1 and C-2, and binds no later
  decision except the one the operator signs (the default wording, below).
- **Implements nothing.** Item 3 of #672 (the ratchet) shipped in #709. The implementation is the
  slice list at the end; the CI wiring of the gate is Zone A and stays the operator's merge.

## Decision

The 182 author-less articles are **not a growing debt but a closed set**: every one is dated
2026-03-01 to 2026-06-06, and no article dated after that lacks an author except four named
transitional rows. So: **(a)** attribute the four transitional rows to a persona only where the
EXEC ledger proves it, **(b)** — *conditional on the ledger dry-run in slice 2 matching none of them* — leave the other 178 with no persona byline in Notion and have the
reader site show a declared, non-persona line for rows dated before `2026-06-02`, and **(c)** turn
the ratchet from "no more than 182" into "none dated on or after 2026-06-02", reported weekly with
the EN-coverage figure. No author is invented anywhere.

## What forced it

Counted from the live manifest (`https://kohuehara.xyz/ai-native-article/posts/manifest.json`,
fetched 2026-10-08, 456 articles):

| slice | articles | no `author` | `hasEn` |
|---|---|---|---|
| dated before 2026-06-02 | 178 | 178 | 34 |
| dated 2026-06-02 or later | 278 | 4 | 260 |
| all | 456 | 182 | 294 |

- **The count is flat because the forward path is closed, not because nothing was done.** Both
  `article-level2/publish-notion.mjs` and `article-level3/publish-notion.mjs` exit 1 without
  `--author`. The first authored row is 2026-06-02; the last author-less row is 2026-06-06. Since
  then 278 articles have been published and none lacks one beyond the four below.
- **The four transitional rows** (2026-06-03 to 06-06) are the only ones that could have a
  persona behind them, because the workforce cadences were then starting:
  `ea210d008234` (analysis), `dca6f71bcedd` and `edf3026b0be8` (explanation/analysis, 06-05),
  `f9d456b22cb0` (analysis).
- **The 178 predate the cadences.** `newsletter/docs/L1-L4-PIPELINE.md` records that L2/L3 used to
  be generated by a Google Apps Script engine on Azure, removed in 2026-06. No persona wrote
  them, so no persona may be named (C-1). **Unverified premise:** that the EXEC ledger would not
  identify any of the 178. The cadences that write engagements did not exist for the old engine, so
  none is expected, but the ledger's start date was not checked here. Decision (b) therefore
  depends on slice 2's dry-run, which runs over **all 182 rows** (not only the four) and reports
  the match count. Any row with exactly one matching engagement is handled like a transitional
  row; if the count is non-zero the plan is re-opened before the operator signs. Running that
  dry-run is a precondition of sign-off.
- **A sentinel "author" would break the reader.** The Notion `Author` column is free text parsed
  as persona slugs (`byline.ts:parseAuthorSlugs`) and resolved against the workforce manifest
  (`findAuthor`); a made-up slug such as `legacy` either renders nothing or implies a persona.
  The roster must not grow a pseudo-persona to hold it (W-5).
- **C-2** puts any real attribution in the Notion row; nothing is edited under
  `newsletter/app/public/posts/`.

## Design

1. **Transitional rows, evidence-gated.** For each of the four slugs, look up the Notion page id
   (the `notionId` in the derived frontmatter) in the EXEC ledger: an `article-level2` or
   `article-level3` engagement whose `artifact.uri` is that page names the agent. Exactly one
   match: write that slug to `Author`. Zero or conflicting matches: the row cannot be proven, and it is **not** silently
   treated as legacy by the date rule, because the four rows are dated 2026-06-03 to 06-06, after
   the cutoff, so the gate in item 4 would fail on them. Treatment: a per-slug exemption list
   (`LEGACY_UNPROVABLE_SLUGS`, at most these four, each with a one-line reason) in the same module
   as the cutoff constant. The list may only shrink. Slice 1 may not merge before slice 2 has
   resolved or listed all four, so the gate cannot land red. The pass is offline, prints its evidence per row, and supports `--dry-run`.
2. **Legacy rows are declared, not filled.** Rows dated before the cutoff keep an empty `Author`.
   The reader site renders, in the byline slot, a fixed line such as
   "Pre-workforce edition — generated by the retired Apps Script pipeline; no persona byline."
   Exact Japanese and English wording is the operator's to sign, and the policy seam with the
   AI-disclosure wording is owned by `wf:kai` (AI-disclosure lane), who signs the declared line
   before slice 3. This sits beside the AI-disclosure
   line (`ai-disclosure-v1.md`), which already applies to every article.
3. **One cutoff constant.** `LEGACY_PIPELINE_CUTOFF = "2026-06-02"` lives in one module imported
   by the gate and the byline renderer, so the policy cannot drift between check and display. It is
   the first date with an authored row, and moving it later is a loosening that needs the
   operator's yes.
4. **Gate.** `check-corpus-author-coverage.mjs` changes from a count ceiling to: no article dated
   on or after the cutoff may lack an author, ceiling 0, except slugs on the exemption list in
   item 1. The legacy set is not counted against it. An article with a missing or unparseable date
   **fails loud** (C-4); it is never assumed legacy.
   The count of legacy rows is printed for information and cannot rise (new articles carry
   authors by construction; a regression would show as a post-cutoff row).
5. **Visibility between letters (item 4).** Report two numbers weekly: forward author coverage
   (278 of 278 once the four are fixed) and EN coverage, split forward (260 of 278) and legacy
   (34 of 178). The home is the existing weekly content-insights job
   (`scripts/content-insights.mjs`, `weekly-content-insights.yml`), not a new workflow; the
   legacy count is shown as a fixed denominator so "unchanged" is expected and a post-cutoff
   miss is the alert.
6. **EN backfill is a separate question.** 144 legacy articles have no English edition. That is
   an L2 generation workload with a cost line (W-3), not a metadata fix, and is not decided here.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Backfill a default persona or "the editorial desk" into Notion.** Rejected: C-1 (it states
  authorship nobody had) and W-5 (a roster entry for a non-persona).
- **A `legacy` sentinel string in `Author`.** Rejected: breaks `findAuthor` and implies a persona
  slug; needs UI special-casing anyway, and then lives in data instead of one constant.
- **Infer authors from style or topic.** Rejected: fabrication by another route.
- **A date-less rule ("empty author means legacy").** Rejected: it would also excuse a new
  author-less row; the cutoff is what keeps the gate strict.
- **Keep the 182 ceiling.** Rejected: it tolerates up to 182 new misses and tells a reader nothing.

## Cost

- One shared constant, one byline branch in the reader (a Zone-A-adjacent change to the byline
  component and a wording decision for the operator).
- A one-time offline lookup against the EXEC ledger and four Notion edits (destructive-Notion
  rules do not apply: the field is empty today, but the operator should still see the dry run).
- The gate becomes slightly more complex (date-aware). The CI wiring remains Zone A.

## Reversal, and what says it was wrong

Remove the byline branch and the cutoff; the Notion rows are unchanged except the four attributed
ones, which are evidence-backed. It was wrong if readers or Kai's AI-disclosure review treat the
declared line as an unacceptable substitute for a byline, or if a post-cutoff row appears with no
author (the gate then fails, which is the point).

## Out of scope

Generating English editions for legacy rows; changing `Author` semantics for multi-author
articles; the AI-disclosure wording; wiring the gate into `deploy-article-site.yml` (Zone A,
operator merge); any edit under `newsletter/app/public/posts/`.

## Implementation slices (each one `issue-implement` PR)

1. `LEGACY_PIPELINE_CUTOFF` module and the gate change in `check-corpus-author-coverage.mjs` with
   tests. Failing cases required: a post-cutoff row with no author fails; a row dated exactly
   2026-06-02 with no author fails (boundary: the cutoff is the first authored date); a row dated
   2026-06-01 with no author passes; a row with a missing or unparseable date fails loud; an
   unprovable transitional slug passes only while on the exemption list. Baseline constant removed.
   Ordered after slice 2.
2. Offline lookup-and-write script, dry-run by default, run over **all 182 rows**, printing the
   EXEC evidence per slug and the total match count; writes only the four transitional rows with
   exactly one match. Ordered first.
3. Reader byline branch for pre-cutoff rows once the operator signs the wording (touches the
   Zone A-adjacent design surface; draft PR only).
4. `content-insights` weekly report: forward author coverage and EN coverage (forward and
   legacy).
5. Operator: wire the gate into `deploy-article-site.yml` (Zone A).
