# plan-write

Hypothesis-as-document. The deliverable reads as a short essay but commits to a falsifiable position.

## Instructions

1. State **one** hypothesis up front (the title is the hypothesis, paraphrased).
2. **Why now** — 1-2 paragraphs of context that triggered this hypothesis being writable today and not three months ago.
3. **Falsifier** — the observable evidence that would force the author to retract. If you cannot name one, the hypothesis is not yet a plan.
4. **Next step if false** — what gets tried instead. Naming this is what separates a plan from a guess.
5. **Next step if true** — 1-2 sentence sketch.

Length target: 600-1200 words in Japanese. ASCII title is acceptable.

## Voice

This skill assumes the PM/Founder voice: confident-but-falsifiable, explicit about cost of being wrong, allergic to "let's see what happens" non-commitments.

## When NOT to use

- The author is reflecting on past decisions (use a different skill — retrospective).
- The claim is purely technical (a design-doc / tech-note is the right shape).
- The intended audience is the operator-of-self only (then a private memory chunk is enough; no published artefact required).
